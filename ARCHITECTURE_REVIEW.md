## Summary
The codebase follows a consistent page-scaffold pattern (ScreenScaffold + useAppState + TAB_ITEMS) with clean separation between pure logic (lib/household.ts, lib/report.ts, lib/streak.ts) and UI, and no circular dependencies were found. The main weaknesses are copy-pasted boilerplate (haptic wrappers, tab arrays, Chip casts, ad-group-id resolution) that bypass existing shared abstractions (useHaptic, lib/env.ts), plus a handful of components/types that appear unused.
